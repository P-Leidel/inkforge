Problem

On the baseline's 144 Hz screen, falling Objects don't look smooth: the world moves in 60 Hz steps and the renderer draws only the latest pose, so each pose shows for two or three frames in an uneven rhythm. The world only answers "everything, now", so the renderer also rebuilds every view each frame and works out what changed itself, with six id maps, two look keys and four sweeps. The verdict and the gallery test each collect the same three id sets. Debris, ghosts and puffs are visual only, yet run in the headless world.

Solution

Each step and each command appends what happened to a list: what was added, what went and why, what cracked, filled, was Released, burst or exploded. The renderer bakes, frees and bursts Debris from that list, and draws each moving body between its poses at the last two steps. Debris, the ghosts and the puffs move to the renderer.

Wins
  • smooth motion at 144 Hz
  • locality: "what changed" decided once
  • renderer drops look keys, sweeps
  • headless world: simulation only
  • Kind loses dropVisuals
  • tests count events, not ids
  • perf R4's pooled Debris starts here
  • M4 kills, drops: one entry each
Seen on the baseline machine: falling Objects don't look smooth at 144 Hz. Reads cost 0.075 ms a frame (Demolition, 331 items), so the rest is for locality, not speed. Replays are safe: drawing between poses never feeds back into the simulation, and Debris draws from its own generator (DEBRIS_SEED), never the simulation's. The render budget test must pass unchanged.
For grilling: drawing between the last two poses shows the world up to one step (16.7 ms) late; decide whether that is fine or whether to extrapolate. A body that is rebuilt, Squeezed or appears mid-step needs one pose, not two. Debris now steps with physics and stops while paused; in the renderer it needs a clock of its own. R, Clear and loading a demo need a "start over" entry.

Problem

Each kind pairs physics.add… with contacts.register, removeBody with unregister and slideOut with squeezed by hand. The Eraser added a seventh way to go and had to call passOnGone after every kind's turn to stay out of the window after a removal. Kind now has 11 members; 24 of its 66 methods are empty.

Solution

Kinds add, slide and remove their bodies through one Arena bodies module. It registers and unregisters each Party, marks a sliding Object Squeezed and passes "gone" on as soon as a body goes. It knows each Party's Colour and role, so it re-applies surfaces after a table edit itself. strokes.ts loses its lifecycle code.

Wins
  • pairs can't come apart
  • no window after a removal
  • enemy kind: one module to call
  • Kind loses applySurfaces
  • strokes.ts sheds its lifecycle
  • removal order tested once
Registration and removal order are part of replay order: keep them. Kinds still never call each other. The rest of #40's strokes.ts split falls out of other tickets: the Squeeze goes with C3, the undo history with #38.

Problem

Six questions about place are answered six ways, and the Eraser added one. Four of them move every body into world space and scan them all, and the Object preview does it again each time a closing Stroke grows. Kind carries solids, surfaceOf and erase only to answer them. src/geometry has no tests of its own.

Solution

One module answers point, overlap, crossing, brush, radius and surface questions over every Party's shapes, with the engine's broadphase as its index, as bodiesWithin already does. The Squeeze moves here from strokes.ts, and M3's overlap charging is one more query.

Wins
  • leverage: one query, seven callers
  • Kind loses solids, surfaceOf, erase
  • Squeeze leaves strokes.ts
  • M3 overlap charging is a query
  • geometry tested at one interface
  • cost follows the neighbourhood
Squeeze results are part of replay order, so world shapes must come out bit-identical. b2World_OverlapCircle is broken in Phaser Box2D 1.1.0: use OverlapAABB, then b2Shape_GetClosestPoint. Strong once the M3 spec includes overlap charging.

Problem

Box2D can't unfreeze a fixed body, so Release, a wake and the end of a slide rebuild it, and every attachment is carried over by hand. bullet still never reaches rebuild. Surfaces are the table's own objects, so an F2 edit reaches a body rebuilt before the next step early.

Solution

The adapter keeps one complete description per body: shapes and added capsules with their own surfaces, density, damping, group, bullet, the never-wake flag and bonds. Creating, rebuilding, setSurface and setMass all apply it.

Wins
  • locality: one record per body
  • drift like bullet can't recur
  • F2 edits apply on the step
  • one rebuild test, every attachment
  • enemy body options land once
  • PhysicsWorld interface unchanged
The order of engine calls in a rebuild (destroy, create, shapes, added shapes, rewelds) is part of replay order. ADR 0001's verdict checks 1, 2 and 4 must read the same.

Also noticed, not candidates
  • The Object preview runs the whole Stroke pipeline, with every kind's solids, whenever the drawn Stroke grows (sandbox-scene.ts 229–240). Its cost grows with Rubble; C3's index answers it.
  • Perf R4 (Debris and Droplets redrawn as Graphics every frame) and R5 (the Stroke preview redraws its whole path) are still open. Both wait on a Demolition reading on the baseline machine with F1 at stats; if it shows no frame over 20 ms, close both.
  • Each Object is baked into a texture of its own, so 100 pebbles are 100 textures (7.2 MB) and, with maxTextures: 1, 100 draw calls. On the baseline that is part of a 2.1 ms render (read with the debug view on). An atlas for Object looks would batch them; do it only if a reading asks.
  • bakedTextureUse() counts in module-level state (baked-textures.ts 20), shared by every scene and every test in a worker. A renderer that isn't destroyed leaks into the next reading.
  • "Already filled" rebuilds every Object view to flash one Outline (sandbox-scene.ts 217). A Fill outcome that carries the Outline, or C1's list, avoids it.
  • strokes.ts is 672 lines with nine concerns now (views, drawing, breaking, the snapshot, the Squeeze, the undo history, Fill, Release, erase). C2, C3 and #38 each take one.
