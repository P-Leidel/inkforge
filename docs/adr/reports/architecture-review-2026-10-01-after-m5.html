Problem

A Level that leaves something out keeps what the last Level left. After Campaign Level 1, the Sandbox and every Gallery demo have no blue, green or red ("Not in this Level"), and Waves stay on. The spec says the sandbox, the gallery and the stress tests have every Colour. What a Level starts with is set in three modules, and the Colours a Level has are read from two sources.

Solution

Loading resolves a Level into everything it starts with: its own value, or the default, never the last Level's. The Session owns the mode (a Campaign Level or free play), and with it Waves, Tanks and the Eraser. The scene's two Sandbox paths become one Session call, and one fact says which Colours are here.

Wins
  • bug fixed: no carry-over
  • locality: a Level's start, one module
  • scene loses its Waves and tool rules
  • Colours here: one source
  • M6's boss Level loads the same way
Confirmed with a scratch run through Session. After playCampaign(0) then play(SANDBOX_LEVEL): game.has('blue') is false and game.waves is true. The Gallery's Bounce demo gives the same. No Session test covers going from a Campaign Level to free play.
For the grilling: should a free-play load keep F2's Waves switch, or turn Waves off unless the Level has Waves? Today F2's Ink table holds both the tuning defaults and the current Level's Tanks, so its Defaults button puts blue into Level 1.

Problem

The bars live in the Defence loop. The Eraser is a separate flag that the Game ignores silently, and the scene and the palette check it again. Whether a reason is a bar or a refusal is decided in four places across three files. Adding "Not in this Level" touched six types. The scene's gates are reachable only through Phaser.

Solution

The Game gives one reading of what the player may do now: which tools are on hand, and for each command the bar, if any. Erasing returns an outcome, as drawing and filling do. One Refusal union has one classifier. The drawing input, the palette and the keys read it.

Wins

  • Eraser: five places → one
  • a new refusal touches one union
  • gates tested headless
