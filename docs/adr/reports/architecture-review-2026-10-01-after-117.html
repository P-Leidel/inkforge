Problem

When the Ink Core is destroyed the Wave stays current, so building stays true. After a loss a Stroke is still accepted and charged, and Fills, the Eraser and undo still work. CONTEXT.md says only R or Clear go on. No test covers it. "What may the player do now" is worked out by the Game from two booleans, and the HUD works out the status from three sources.

Solution

The Defence loop gets a lost phase. It answers one question, what the player may do now, and the Game and drawing input ask only that. The HUD draws the phase from the reading. The Waves switch goes through the Game, which then stops handing out defence for writing.

Wins
  • locality: permissions in one module
  • bug fixed: no building after a loss
  • Campaign gets a lost outcome
  • provisional rules: one place to flip
  • HUD reads one phase
Confirmed with a scratch run: after the Ink Core's HP reaches 0 during a Wave, game.submitStroke returns line and charges the Tank. The cause is defence-loop.ts:258: if (this.coreDestroyed) return this.world.pause(); leaves current set.

Problem

The retry point is spread over the Game's snapshot, the loop's retryIndex and the world's snapshot. A callback order in space keeps them in step. Game.load pauses the world itself when Waves are on. The forget-everything list is written twice, and the start-over copy is reached only by tests, because load and reset swallow that entry.

Solution

The Defence loop snapshots and restores its own position, as the Ink Tanks do. space says start, resume or pause, and the Game carries it out. The Game takes one checkpoint in one place and restores it in one place. retryIndex, the second forget list and the phase decision in load go.

Wins
  • locality: R in one module
  • ordering rule becomes code
  • delete a test-only branch
  • M5 pick and Tanks roll back free
Milestone 5 adds state that R has to bring back: a reward picked in the Intermission and per-Level Tank maximums. With three snapshots, each addition touches all three modules.

Problem

Clear, and the current Level, exist only as a closure in the Phaser scene, so they can't be tested headless (waves.test.ts:245 rebuilds Clear by hand). A Level can't say it is played in Waves. A Level's Tanks carry over into the next Level that has none. F2 is kept in step two different ways.

Solution

A module in src/game/ holds what is being played: one Level, or the Campaign and where in it. It does clear, next and back to the sandbox, decides Waves on or off and the Tanks per Level, and sends one change signal. The scene's buttons forward to it.

Wins

  • leverage: Campaign is data again
  • no Tanks carry over by accident
  • F2 sync: two mechanisms → one
Its shape is milestone 5's Campaign, so settle it while grilling the M5 spec, not as a refactor before it. Its name should come from CONTEXT.md (Campaign, Level) or join it.
