Fix before milestone 6: from the code review of #171–#176
  • Bug: a Run's tiles can be drawn with another Run's pose. In lines-drawing.ts:110–129, baked tiles are matched to Runs by their position in line.runs, and thrown away only when the number of Runs changes. But Lines.dropPiece re-sorts the Runs by their first Piece (lines.ts:569).
    • Example: a Line has a Grounded Run [0,4,5,6] and a fallen Run [2]. When Piece 0 goes, the Grounded Run becomes [4,5,6] and moves behind [2]. There are still two Runs, so nothing is unbaked.
    • The fallen Run then gets the Grounded Run's world-space tiles, and the Grounded tiles move with the fallen body.
    • Fix: key tiles by each Run's first Piece, or unbake when the order changes.
  • Bug, likely: a Blast can hit a Line twice when it collapses. reform rebuilds bodies through removeBody(…, 'undone'), and ArenaBodies.removeBody calls gone(parties) (arena-bodies.ts:481). Blasts.gone (blasts.ts:186) is not lifted the way Bonds and Patches are, so it forgets acted for Parties that are kept.
    • Example: a Blast breaks a shelf's foot. The step's Collapse reforms the rest, and the ring, still spreading, damages those Pieces again.
    • The contact ledger's unregister resets their contact history the same way.
    • C4 fixes this whole class at once.
  • Bug, minor: Bonds can land in the wrong order. Bonds.land splices each Bond back at its place from before the lift (bonds.ts:166). If one lifted Bond is skipped because its host is gone, the ones after it land one slot off. A cap's "oldest first" then removes the wrong Bond.
  • Glossary: lowercase "run". lines.ts uses lowercase run for a connected group of Pieces: collapse's doc and its local run at 655–709, and cutOff's runs: Piece[][] at 720–746. CONTEXT.md reserves Run, and its Collapse entry says "connected stretches of Pieces" for exactly this reason. Use stretch in the code too.
  • Doc slip. In arena-query.ts:54–57, Erasable was inserted under the comment /** An Object, as a query names it. */. That leaves two doc comments stacked, and FoundObject without its own.

Problem

About 30 places in 7 files assume an Enemy is one upright box. The Siege Walker has legs and has to be tripped, and it has nothing to plug into. A Blast would hurt it once for each leg.

Solution

Put the body behind a small interface that the Enemy rules ask: where its feet are, what is ahead of it and touching it, its outline, its Parties. Today's box is one adapter. The Siege Walker is the second, built on the one-body-many-Parties path that loose Runs already use.

Benefits
  • two adapters: a real seam
  • leverage: the rules stay as they are for the boss
  • damage once per Enemy
  • tests: one fake body

This keeps ADRs 0010, 0011 and 0013 as they are. Take it into the milestone 6 spec rather than doing it before.

Lines · after any Piece goes
split each Run into connected stretchesthe Grounded stretch stays fixedothers keep their form: Frozen stays Frozen, fallen stays fallenreuses reform: Parties, Bonds, Patches kept
Problem

Only the Grounded Run is ever split. A Frozen or fallen Run that loses a middle Piece stays one rigid body, made of two pieces held together across empty space. The two forms behave differently for no reason a player can see.

Solution

After a Piece goes, Lines splits any Run with a gap into its connected stretches, through the reform path it already has. The grounding walk and the connectivity walk become one.

Benefits
  • locality: "what holds together" answered once
  • Runs behave alike in every form
  • tests: a few cases in collapse.test.ts

For the grilling: is the gap intended? "One rigid body" could allow it, but "a stretch" suggests not. Should a Frozen stretch cut off its Frozen Line stay Frozen, or fall? Either answer needs the Run entry in CONTEXT.md to say it.

Problem

C1 asked for a change of form to keep the Parties and run quietly. Today it keeps the Parties, but every kind still hears them as gone. Only the two kinds that rehost knows to lift are shielded. The Blast double hit is one consequence. The next kind with per-Party state will be another.

Solution

Arena bodies gets one way to rebuild a body under the same Parties that no kind hears as a removal. A kind that holds something on the body hears that it moved. Lift and land in Bonds and Patches shrink to re-attaching.

Benefits
  • locality: "Parties kept" means kept, everywhere
  • the double hit and the Bond-order slip go
  • deletion test: rehost and two lift/land pairs collapse into one rebuild
  • tests: a Blast plus Collapse case in collapse.test.ts
