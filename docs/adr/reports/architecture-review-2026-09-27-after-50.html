Problem

Five questions about place are answered five ways. Four of them move every body into world space and scan it, and the Object preview does that each frame a closing Stroke grows, so its cost grows with the Rubble. Kind carries solids and erase only to answer them, and 6 of those 12 methods are empty. Milestone 3's overlap charging is a sixth question.

Solution

Now that Arena bodies knows every body's and every added shape's owner, one module answers all six questions over those shapes, with the broadphase as its index. Callers ask it. The Squeeze stays a rule that calls the crossing query. The geometry gets its tests first, at this module's interface.

Wins
  • leverage: one query, six callers
  • Kind loses solids, erase
  • M3 overlap charging is a query
  • geometry tested at one interface
  • preview cost follows the neighbourhood
  • strokes.ts sheds three concerns

The ticket is filed and ready-for-agent. Its blocker, #40, closed with #54. The ticket's criteria still hold: pin shortestWayOut and the overlap tests first, since Squeeze results are part of replay order; the brush must still reach Patches, which bodiesWithin skips; b2World_OverlapCircle is broken in Phaser Box2D 1.1.0. Arena bodies already has the surface lookup the ticket moved out of this module (surfaceOf).

Problem

Five functions work out how much Ink an Outline or a Fill is, each with its own copy of the formula. Milestone 3 adds four more: what a Stroke and a Fill cost, what undo refunds, and overlap charging. The Sandbox world's commands don't say what they spent, and undo returns nothing, so a refund has nothing to go on.

Solution

One module says how much Ink a Line, an Outline and a Fill are, by Colour. Mass, Blasts, Spills and Rubble read from it. submitStroke, fillAt and undo report the Ink they spent or gave back, so the Ink Tanks can sit wherever #38 puts the Build Phase without measuring anything again.

Wins
  • locality: one Ink formula
  • weight always follows Ink spent
  • refund equals cost, by construction
  • Ink Tanks test on outcomes alone
  • #38 decides placement, not measuring

Doesn't reopen #38. Where the phase and the Ink Tanks live stays that ticket's decision for the milestone 3 spec. This candidate only makes sure each answer finds the same numbers. Overlap charging needs C1's "lying on a Line" query. Mass feeds replays, so the new measure must give bit-identical masses.

Problem

The scene decides what a press, a drag and a release mean. That covers click or Stroke, Eraser chunks, Release, the refusal preview and what to flash, and none of it has tests. The small checks were split out and tested alone, but the order they're called in is where the rules live. Milestone 3 adds Core Zone and Wave Ink refusals here.

Solution

A headless module takes pointer and tool events, issues Sandbox world commands, and says what the preview and the flash show. The Phaser scene only forwards events and draws what it is told. Tests drive it with sample paths.

Wins
  • input rules tested without Phaser
  • locality: gesture rules in one module
  • M3 refusals land in tested code
  • scene shrinks to forwarding
  • demos could replay real gestures

For grilling: whether the module also owns the refusal preview's throttle (once per new sample count) or leaves frame timing to the scene; and whether M3's Core Zone check belongs here or in the Sandbox world, where a Stroke from a demo would meet it too.

Problem

The world calls six rule phases and three kind calls in an order that must never change, and the rules' own tests can't check that order. Each phase needs its caller to know the order. Milestone 4 adds more phases: enemies pressing on Lines, kills and Ink drops.

Solution

The Material rules take the step's report and run every consequence in their own order. They reach the kinds through the Arena port they already have. The world keeps only physics, the snapshot and each kind's step.

Wins
  • interface: six phases become one
  • order tested at the rules' interface
  • M4 phases land in one module

Speculative because today the order changes rarely, the rules already have fakes of both ports, and the Arena port would grow by three methods (the objects that stick, the gluers, the Blasts' spread). Revisit when the milestone 4 spec names its enemy phases.

Also noticed, not candidates
  • The Sandbox world has two read interfaces for the same views: contents (14 reads) and seven getters, lines to blasts (282 reads, mostly tests). Keep one when a ticket already touches both.
  • Of Kind's 42 methods across six kinds, 14 are empty: gone in 3, erase in 2, solids in 4, step in 5. C1 removes 6 of them. The rest are the price of one fixed order over every kind, which replays need.
  • strokes.ts is still 652 lines. C1 takes objectAt, erase and solids, and #38 takes the undo history. What stays (Lines, Objects, Fill, Release, breaking, the Squeeze) belongs together.
  • Breakable is shaped like a Stroke: a Colour and a role pick its numbers from the material table. Milestone 4's enemies take damage by type, not by Colour, so the Material rules' T will need to widen. That's a question for the milestone 4 spec, not a refactor now.
