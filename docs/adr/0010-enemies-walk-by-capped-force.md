# Enemies walk by a capped force, not a set velocity

An Enemy is an upright, non-rotating body pushed toward its walking speed by a horizontal force that never exceeds its type's walkForce. We rejected setting its velocity every step: it is simpler, but it overrides everything else acting on the body, so glue drag, blue bounces, Blasts and blows would do nothing to a walking Enemy. We also rejected a motor joint, because it adds joints and engine surface ([ADR 0003](0003-no-joints-in-mvp.md)). With a capped force, "green slows Heavies less" ([ADR 0007](0007-glue-drags-every-moving-body.md)), "Runners fly off blue" and "Heavies shove Objects" come from the simulation, and an Enemy pressing against something is simply one at its walkForce limit that isn't moving.
